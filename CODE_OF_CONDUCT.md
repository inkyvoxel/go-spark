# Code of Conduct

Be nice.

The slightly longer version:

* Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
* Disagree with ideas, not with people. No personal attacks, harassment, or
  discrimination.
* Assume good intent, and remember there is a person on the other end.

If someone is not being nice, email hello@inkyvoxel.com. The maintainer will
look into it and may hide or remove content, lock threads, or block repeat
offenders. That's it — small project, simple rules.

**Using this template for your own project?** This file is yours now. Keep
it, rewrite it, or replace it with something more formal (such as the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)) as your
community grows.
